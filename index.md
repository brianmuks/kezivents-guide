# Kezivents User Guide

Kezivents lets you create a beautiful digital invitation for your wedding, party or traditional ceremony, invite every guest by WhatsApp or email, collect RSVPs, and check guests in at the door with a QR code. This guide walks through everything, from creating your account to welcoming your guests on the day.

**Who this guide is for:** hosts, couples, event planners and anyone helping them. No technical knowledge is needed.

**How to use this guide:** each section stands on its own and starts with a one-line summary, so you can jump straight to what you need. The last sections are for people making videos or marketing material about Kezivents.

## 1. Kezivents at a glance

**In short:** Kezivents is a digital invitation and guest management platform for weddings, parties and traditional ceremonies.

With Kezivents you can:

- **Design an invitation** from ready-made templates, with your names, photos, story, timeline, map and background song.
- **Invite guests** one by one, or hundreds at once from a spreadsheet.
- **Send invitations** by WhatsApp or email, automatically or from your own phone.
- **Take RSVPs**, so people can ask to attend and you approve them, or approve them automatically.
- **Track responses**: see who is attending, who said no, and who hasn't answered.
- **Check guests in** at the entrance and at meal time by scanning the QR code on their invitation.
- **Get notified** when someone RSVPs, and get a daily reminder email while RSVPs wait for your approval.

Every guest gets a **personal invitation link**. Opening it shows their own invitation card, with their name, number of tickets, seat, a QR code for entry, and a downloadable card.

On Freemium events, the bottom of the invitation also shows "Planning an event of your own? Create one free with 25 invites". Paid events show only "Powered by Kezivents".

What needs a paid plan:

- On the due date, if a balance is still open, you get a friendly **reminder email**. It repeats weekly a few times at most.
- When the last payment clears the balance, you get a receipt saying your plan is **fully paid**.
- Scan the QR code on any receipt to confirm it's genuine.

## 3. Create your account and verify your email

**In short:** sign up with your name, email, phone number and a password, then enter the 6-digit code we email you.

1. Go to the Kezivents website and choose **Sign up** (or **Get started for free**).
2. Fill in your **first name**, **last name**, **email address**, **phone number** and a **password** (at least 5 characters, with letters and numbers).
3. Press **Create account**. You'll see "Account created successfully! We sent a verification code to your email."
4. Open your email and find the **verification code** (a number such as 123456).
5. Enter it on the **Verify your email** screen and press **Verify email**.
6. You'll see "Email verified successfully. You can now sign in."

Good to know:

- **Didn't get the code?** Check your spam folder, then press **Resend code**.
- **Codes expire.** If you see "Invalid or expired code", request a new one.
- **Verification is required to sign in with your password.** If you try to sign in before verifying, Kezivents sends you a fresh code automatically.
- Each email address and phone number can only be used for one account.

## 4. Signing in

**In short:** sign in with your password, or with a one-time code sent to your email.

- **With your password:** enter your email and password and press **Sign in**.
- **With an email code:** choose the **Email code** tab, enter your email, press **Send code**, then enter the code and press **Sign in with code**. This is handy when you can't remember your password.
- **Forgot your password?** Press **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and follow the reset link we send you.

After signing in you land on your **dashboard**. The menu on the left takes you to **Home**, **Notifications**, **Events**, **Guests**, **Services** and **Plans**.

## 5. Create your event

**In short:** open Events, press Create event, fill in the details and choose a template.

1. In the menu, open **Events** and press **Create event**.
2. Choose the **event type**: **Wedding**, **Party** or **Traditional** (for traditional ceremonies).
3. Pick an **invitation template**:
   - Weddings: **Standard**, **Vintage Classic**, **Noir Bloom** or **Gilded Frame** (your photos in gold picture frames).
   - Parties: **Standard** or **Stylish**.
   - Traditional: **Ngoma**, with Ngoma drums guests can tap to play, chitenge prints and a firelit hero.

   To see real examples before choosing, open **Templates** on the Kezivents website. Each template has a **View sample** link.
4. Fill in the main details:
   - **Names:** for weddings, the groom's and bride's full names. You can also set an optional **Wedding title** that replaces the names on the invitation.
   - **Event code:** a short unique code such as BO-2026. It's created for you from the couple's initials and the year; on Silver, Gold and Business you can choose your own. It becomes part of your event page, RSVP and guest links, so it **can't be changed after the event is created**. Editing names or anything else never changes it.
   - **Date and time** of the event. Pick them from the calendar and the hour and minute columns, or type them. Dates show as, for example, **14 Nov 2026**, and times are always 24-hour, for example **14:30**, on every phone and computer.
   - **Host / organizer** and an optional **Contact number**, shown to guests for enquiries.
   - **Story** or **Event description**, an optional **Gift message** and **Dress code** (with an optional outfit picture).
   - **Children allowed:** turn this off to show a "Strictly no children" notice.
   - **Guest contribution** and **currency**, shown on each card. Set it to 0 to hide it.
5. Add **photos** (up to 5; Freemium events allow fewer). Use the sliders to keep faces inside the crop. Images under 1 MB load fastest for guests.
6. Build the **timeline**: each item has a time, a name (Ceremony, Reception…), a location and an optional description. Use **Search or choose on map** to pin the venue, so guests get a **View map** and **Open in Google Maps** button.
7. Choose the **Visibility**:
   - **Public:** the event page can be opened by anyone with the link.
   - **Private:** only you can open the event page. Personal invitation links you send to guests still work.
8. Press **Save**. You'll see "Event saved successfully."

Good to know:

- **Editing locks 48 hours before the event** and after it ends, so details don't change at the last minute.
- **Changing the event type of a saved event** (for example from Wedding to Traditional) switches it to the new type's default template and **resets every invitation setting** you customised: texts, colours and options. Kezivents asks you to confirm first. Links you already shared keep working; they take guests to the new design.
- **Freemium accounts can create one event.** Upgrade to Business to manage up to 50.
- **Duplicate an event** to start a new one from an existing event: press **Duplicate** on the event in **Events** (or in its details window). The Create event form opens with everything filled in except the **date** and **event code**. Tick **Also copy the guest list** to bring the guests along as new invitations (new links, Pending, no RSVP answers or history). Guests already on the new list, RSVPs you never approved, and guests beyond your plan's invite limit aren't copied.

## 6. Customise your invitation (Advanced settings)

**In short:** Advanced settings let you change the invitation's wording, options, colours, intro video and background song.

In the event form, open **Advanced settings**. It has these sections:

- **Hero section** (Gilded Frame and Vintage Classic only): settings for the top of the invitation, the first thing guests see.
  - **Gilded Frame:** upload **Your own frame** to replace the gold frame around your photos. Choose **Photos in the frame**: **Changing** fades through all your photos, **Static** keeps photo 1 in the frame. Photo 1 is the photo you numbered 1 in Photos.
  - **Vintage Classic:** photo style (black and white or colour), photo carousel, name layout and the extra text lines.
- **Text:** the intro line ("Together with their families"), the hero invite text, the scroll button text and the contribution label. Clear a field to hide that line.
- **Options:**
  - **Show countdown** and **Show contribution**.
  - **Include guest code in invitation messages.**
  - **Auto-scroll the invitation** (on by default) and its **Scrolling speed**: Slow, Normal or Fast. See [What your guests see](#17-what-your-guests-see).
  - **Children allowed**, **Allow RSVP**, **Auto-approve RSVP requests**, **RSVP deadline** and **Allow well wishes** (see [RSVP](#14-rsvp-let-guests-request-an-invitation)).
- **Colour / theme:** the primary and secondary colours of the invitation.
- **Video (paid):** an intro video that plays before guests see the invitation. Use a Google Drive share link or a direct MP4 under 3 MB.
- **Song (paid):** search for a song or artist and attach a preview track. You can set the volume and turn autoplay on. Please make sure you have permission to use the song.
- **Bridal party (Silver, Premium, Gold):** add up to 7 people with photos and titles such as Best man or Bridesmaid.

Each section has **Reset to default** if you want to start again.

## 7. View your event page

**In short:** open your event and press View event page to see the invitation exactly as guests will.

1. Open **Events** and press **View** on your event.
2. The event details window has quick buttons: **Guests**, **View event page**, **Copy event page link**, **Scan for Entry**, **Scan for Food**, **Review well wishes** (when well wishes are on) and **Edit**.
3. Press **View event page**. It opens your invitation without any one guest's details.
4. To share it, press **Copy event page link** and paste the link wherever you like. You don't need to open the page first. If the event is private and RSVP is off, you're reminded that only you can open the link.

Who should get which link:

- The **event page** is the general invitation. When RSVP is on, it has an **RSVP now** button, so this is the link you share in group chats or on social media.
- Each guest's **personal invitation link** shows their own card, tickets and QR code. You send these from the guest list.

## 8. Add guests one by one

**In short:** open Guests, choose your event, press Add Guest and enter their name and WhatsApp number.

1. In the menu, open **Guests** and select your event at the top.
2. Press **Add Guest**.
3. Fill in:
   - **First name** and **Last name** (required).
   - **WhatsApp number** with the country code, for example +260 977 000 000 (required).
   - **Email address** (optional; needed for email invitations).
   - **Seat number** (optional), for example "Table 4, Seat 2".
   - **Slots / tickets:** how many people this card admits. A couple sharing one card would have 2.
   - **Contribution override** and **Currency override** (optional): a different amount for this guest only. Leave blank to use the event's amount.
   - **Card accent color** (optional): a colour just for this guest's card.
4. Press **Add Guest**.

From the guest list you can then, for each guest:

- **Copy the link to their card** or **share** it.
- Open their **details** (tap the guest, or press **View details**) to see how their invitation went out, and press **Preview card** to see it as they will. A preview is just for you: opening it, or answering "Will you be attending?" in it, doesn't change the guest's status.
- **Edit** or **delete** the guest.
- **Reset RSVP** if they answered "not attending" and changed their mind.

The top of the guest list shows how many **tickets are allocated** and how many **WhatsApp credits are left** for the event.

## 9. Import and export guests with a CSV file

**In short:** download the template, fill it in with a spreadsheet, and import hundreds of guests at once. This needs Standard or above.

**Import guests:**

1. In **Guests**, select your event and press **Import / export**.
2. Next to the template, press **Download** to get a CSV file with the right columns.
3. Open it in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers and fill in one guest per row. The columns are:

   | Column | Required | Example |
   |---|---|---|
   | firstName | Yes | Mary |
   | lastName | Yes | Banda |
   | phoneNumber | Yes | +260977000000 |
   | email | No | mary@example.com |
   | seatNumber | No | Table 1 |
   | ticketsAllocated | No | 1 |
   | notes | No | Bride's aunt |

4. Save it as **CSV**.
5. Back in Kezivents, press **Choose file** and pick your CSV.
6. Choose **Import only**, or **Import and send emails** to email every imported guest who has an email address.
7. Check the preview. Rows with problems are marked, for example a missing name or phone, an invalid number, or a duplicate phone or email. Those rows are skipped.
8. Press **Confirm import**. You'll see how many guests were added and how many failed.

Good to know:

- **Phone numbers need the country code** (start with +).
- **Plan limits apply.** If an import goes over your plan's guest limit, the extra rows are skipped. Upgrade first if you need more.
- **WhatsApp isn't sent automatically on import.** Send WhatsApp invitations afterwards from the guest list (see [section 12](#12-send-invitations-automatically-by-whatsapp-or-email)).

**Export guests:** press **Import / export**, then **Export**, to download your whole guest list as a spreadsheet.

## 10. Guest groups: shared settings for groups of guests

**In short:** put guests into groups such as "Bride's family" and give the whole group the same tickets, contribution and card colour. This needs Standard or above.

1. In **Guests**, select your event and press **Groups**.
2. Press **Create group**.
3. Give it a **Group name**, for example "Bride's family" or "Work friends".
4. Set any of these, which then apply to every member of the group:
   - **Tickets allocated:** how many people each member's card admits.
   - **Contribution amount** and **Currency:** a different contribution for this group.
   - **Card color:** a card colour for the group.
   - **Show on RSVP form** (off by default): see below.
5. Press **Save**.
6. Under **Available guests**, tick the guests to include and press **Add selected**. To take someone out, tick them under **Current members** and press **Remove selected**.

Good to know:

- **Use groups to charge different amounts.** For example, family can contribute less than colleagues.
- **Let RSVP guests sort themselves.** Turn on **Show on RSVP form** for groups such as "Bride's family" and "Groom's family". The RSVP form then asks **"Which group are you with?"**, guests must pick one, and they join that group with its settings. The group's extra tickets only apply while your event is within its guest limit.
- **A group can only be deleted when it has no members.**
- **For a single person,** use the guest's own **Contribution override** instead (see [section 8](#8-add-guests-one-by-one)).

## 11. Invite guests manually with WhatsApp (free)

**In short:** press the green WhatsApp icon next to a guest to open WhatsApp on your phone with the invitation message ready to send. It costs no credits.

1. In **Guests**, find the guest.
2. Press the green **WhatsApp** icon ("Invite manually with WhatsApp").
3. WhatsApp opens with the guest's number and a ready-written message containing their personal invitation link.
4. Press send in WhatsApp.

Good to know:

- **It's free and works on every plan,** including Freemium, because the message is sent from your own WhatsApp.
- **Kezivents remembers you shared it.** The guest is marked "Shared by you on WhatsApp" with the date and how many times you opened it.
- **It's best for small numbers of guests.** For many guests, use automatic sending.

## 12. Send invitations automatically by WhatsApp or email

**In short:** select guests, press Send via WhatsApp or Send via email, review, and Kezivents sends them for you. WhatsApp uses one credit per message.

**Send to many guests at once:**

1. In **Guests**, select your event.
2. Tick the guests you want to invite, or press **Select up to 500**.
3. Press **Send via WhatsApp** or **Send via email**.
4. A confirmation window shows how many guests are **not invited** yet, **previously sent**, **manually opened**, **missing contact** details or **already queued**. For WhatsApp it also shows how many **credits** it will use and how many you have left.
5. Press **Send only not invited** to skip anyone already contacted, or **Resend to all** to send again to everyone selected.

**Send to one guest:** open the guest's details and use **Send WhatsApp invitation** or **Send email invitation**.

**Track delivery:** each guest shows their WhatsApp status (**Waiting to send**, **Sending**, **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Read**, **Failed to send**) and whether an email was sent. Use the **Invitation delivery** filter to find guests who haven't been contacted or whose message failed. The dashboard also warns you when guests haven't received a WhatsApp invitation.

Good to know:

- **Automatic WhatsApp needs a paid plan and credits.** Email invitations need the guest's email address.
- **Every WhatsApp message uses one credit, including resends.** Use **Send only not invited** to avoid paying twice.

## 13. WhatsApp credits and how to buy more

**In short:** one credit sends one WhatsApp invitation. Paid plans include credits, and you can buy more for any event.

How credits work:

- **Credits belong to an event,** not your account. If you have two events, each has its own balance.
- **Your plan includes credits for each event:** Standard 50, Silver 100, Premium 150, Gold 250, Business 50 per event. Freemium events can't send automatic WhatsApp messages.
- **Credits are used for every automatic WhatsApp message:**
  - invitations you send from the guest list, including resends;
  - approving an RSVP with **WhatsApp invitation** ticked;
  - invitations sent automatically to auto-approved RSVPs (see [section 15](#15-approving-rsvps-automatic-or-manual)).
- **Manual WhatsApp sharing from your phone and email invitations never use credits.**
- **Your balance** shows at the top of the guest list as "WhatsApp credits left", with how many were included, added and used.

**Credit prices:**

| Bundle | Price | Per credit |
|---|---|---|
| 100 credits | K150 | K1.50 |
| 250 credits | K350 | K1.40 |
| 500 credits | K650 | K1.30 |

Other amounts are K1.50 per credit.

**How to buy credits:**

1. In **Guests**, select the event that needs credits.
2. Press **Buy credits**.
3. Choose a bundle, then press **Order on WhatsApp** (or **Call**). A message is prepared with the number of credits, the price and your event's name and code.
4. Pay as agreed with the Kezivents team, for example by mobile money.
5. Once payment is received, the credits are added to that event and appear in your balance.

## 14. RSVP: let guests request an invitation

**In short:** turn on RSVP, share your event page, and people fill in a short form to ask for their invitation. This needs Standard or above.

RSVP is for when you don't have everyone's details in advance. Instead of adding each guest yourself, you share one link and guests add themselves.

**Turn it on:**

1. Edit your event and open **Advanced settings → Options**.
2. Turn on **Allow RSVP**.
3. Choose whether to **Auto-approve RSVP requests** (see [section 15](#15-approving-rsvps-automatic-or-manual)).
4. Optionally set an **RSVP deadline** (Silver, Premium, Gold). After it passes, the RSVP button stays but tells guests the deadline has passed.
5. Press **Save**.

**Share it:** open the event, press **Copy event page link**, and paste the link into WhatsApp groups, social media or anywhere else.

**What guests do:**

1. They open your event page and press **RSVP now** ("Will you celebrate with us?"). The form can also pop up on its own after they've looked at the invitation.
2. They enter their **full name**, **phone number with country code** and, optionally, their **email** and a **well wish**. If you show groups on the RSVP form, they also tap the group they're with (see [Guest groups](#10-guest-groups-shared-settings-for-groups-of-guests)).
3. They press **Submit RSVP**.

Good to know:

- **Each phone number or email can RSVP once per event.** Someone who tries again is told they've already responded.
- **Guests can choose "Remind me later"** when the form pops up, and it comes back later.

## 15. Approving RSVPs: automatic or manual

**In short:** with auto-approval, guests get their invitation straight away. With manual approval, you review each request first.

### Auto-approval

When **Auto-approve RSVP requests** is on:

1. A guest submits the RSVP form.
2. They're approved immediately and see "Your RSVP is approved", with their personal invitation link to copy or share. Then they're taken to their invitation.
3. **If the event has WhatsApp credits,** Kezivents also sends the invitation to the guest's WhatsApp automatically. The guest sees "We're also sending your invitation to your WhatsApp."

What it costs:

- **Each automatic WhatsApp invitation uses one credit** from the event.
- **No credits left?** No WhatsApp is sent, but the guest still gets their link on screen.
- **There's a safety limit of 60 automatic WhatsApp sends per event per hour,** so a flood of fake RSVPs can't drain your credits.
- **The tradeoff:** anyone with your event page link can get an invitation. Use auto-approval when you're happy for the link to be open, for example a public celebration.

### Manual approval

When auto-approval is off:

1. The guest submits the form and is told their request is waiting for the host's approval.
2. You're notified: the request appears in **Notifications**, the **dashboard** shows how many RSVPs are waiting, and you get a **daily reminder email** while any are pending (see [section 16](#16-notifications-and-reminder-emails)).
3. Open **Guests** for that event. Filter by **Awaiting RSVP approval** to see only pending requests.
4. Check the person, then press **Approve RSVP**.
5. In the **Approve RSVP** window, choose how they get their invitation:
   - **Email invitation** (if they gave an email);
   - **WhatsApp invitation:** uses one credit, and the window shows your remaining credits and the balance after sending.
6. Press **Approve and notify**, or **Approve without notifying** if you'll send the link yourself.

Changed your mind? Use **Move to pending** to take an approval back.

**Which should I choose?**

| | Auto-approval | Manual approval |
|---|---|---|
| Guest waits | No, instant | Until you approve |
| You control who comes | No | Yes |
| WhatsApp credits | Used automatically for each RSVP | Used only when you tick WhatsApp |
| Best for | Open events, large parties | Weddings, private or limited-space events |

## 16. Notifications and reminder emails

**In short:** Kezivents tells you when guests RSVP and reminds you daily while RSVPs wait for your approval.

**Notifications page:**

- Open **Notifications** in the menu. A red number shows how many are unread.
- Each RSVP appears as "Name responded to Event", with the time and its status: **Awaiting your approval**, **Approved**, **Approved automatically** or **Removed from the guest list**.
- New ones are marked **New**. Press **Review** to open that event's guest list and approve the guest. Press **Mark all as read** to clear the count.

**Dashboard:**

- The **RSVP approvals** card shows how many RSVPs are waiting for approval in each event, each with a **Review** button.
- It also shows your unread notification count.

**Daily reminder email:**

- If any RSVPs are waiting for your approval on an upcoming event, you get one email a day listing how many are waiting per event, with a **Review RSVPs** button.
- The emails stop once you've approved everyone, or once the event is over.

## 17. What your guests see

**In short:** each guest opens their personal link to see their invitation, confirm attendance and download a card with their QR code.

1. The guest opens their personal link from WhatsApp, email or wherever you shared it.
2. If you added one, the **intro video** plays, then the invitation opens with your photos, names, story, countdown, timeline, map and music.
   - With **Auto-scroll** on, the invitation glides gently down on its own so guests see everything. It starts after the top of the invitation has been on screen for at least 10 seconds, or after every photo there has been shown once if your photos rotate.
   - Any touch pauses it and it carries on after a few seconds. Guests can stop it completely with the small **pause** button, and it never runs for guests whose phone asks for reduced motion.
3. Their **personal details** show: their name, number of tickets, seat, contribution and dress code.
4. About 10 seconds after the invitation opens (after the intro video, if there is one), or when they press **Download Invitation**, they're asked **"Will you be attending?"**:
   - **Yes, I'll be attending:** they download their card with the **QR code** for entry.
   - **No, I won't be attending:** their answer is saved for you and the entry code is hidden.
   - **Ask me later:** the question comes back a little later (after 30 seconds, then a little longer each time, up to every 2 minutes), since you need everyone's answer.
5. The **Status** column on your guest list follows each invitation:
   - **Pending:** the invitation was created or sent, or the guest filled in the RSVP form, but they haven't opened their invitation yet. RSVPs you haven't approved yet also show **Awaiting approval**.
   - **Viewed:** the guest opened their own invitation link. Your own previews don't count.
   - **Attending** or **Not attending:** the guest answered "Will you be attending?".
   - **Checked in:** an extra chip that appears once they're scanned in at the entrance.

   You can filter the list by any of these. A guest's details also show when they first opened their invitation.

If a guest said no and changes their mind, press **Reset RSVP** on their row so they can answer again.

## 18. Guests who lost their invitation link

**In short:** share your event's guest code; guests enter it with their phone number to get their invitation link back.

1. In **Guests**, select your event and press **Guest code** in the toolbar.
2. Copy the **Guest invitation code** and share it with your guests, for example in your WhatsApp group.
3. A guest who lost their link opens the event page. Under "Already responded and lost your invitation link?", in the RSVP section or the RSVP form, they press **Retrieve my invitation**.
4. They enter the **phone number** they used and the **code from your host**.
5. If they're approved, they get their invitation link again. If their RSVP is still waiting, they're told it hasn't been approved yet.

If the code ends up somewhere it shouldn't, press **New code**. The old code stops working straight away.

## 19. On the day: scanning guests in

**In short:** open Scan for Entry on a phone and scan each guest's QR code at the door; use Scan for Food at meal time.

1. Open your event and press **Scan for Entry** (or **Scan for Food**).
2. Allow the camera when your phone asks.
3. Point the camera at the QR code on the guest's card.
4. You'll see the guest's name and how many of their slots have been used, for example "1 / 2 used, 1 remaining".

**No QR code to scan?** Every guest has a 4-digit **ticket number**, shown on their invitation and in your guest list (for example **No. 1229**). In the scanner, type it into **Ticket number (last 4 digits)** instead. Guests who used the RSVP form before October 2026 may have a 6-digit number; scan their QR code instead.

Good to know:

- **Each card admits as many people as its slots / tickets.** When they're all used, you'll see "Entry slots have been exhausted for this guest card".
- **Meal scans work the same way,** so each guest is served the right number of meals.
- **The dashboard tracks totals:** entry scans, food scans and RSVP responses.
- **Gold plan events can have professional doormen** handle check-in for you.

## 20. Well wishes

**In short:** guests can leave a short message for you, which appears on your invitation once you approve it. This needs Silver, Premium or Gold.

1. Edit your event and turn on **Allow well wishes** in **Advanced settings → Options**.
2. Guests can write a message on the invitation page, or add one to their RSVP.
3. Open the event and press **Review well wishes** to **Approve**, **Reject** or **Delete** each message. Only approved messages appear on your invitation.

## 21. Frequently asked questions

**Is Kezivents free?**
Yes, to start. Freemium gives you one event with 25 invitations at no cost. Paid plans start at K350 per event.

**Do my guests need to install an app or create an account?**
No. Guests just open a link in their phone's browser.

**What's the difference between the event page and a personal invitation link?**
The event page is the general invitation you can share with anyone. With RSVP on, it's where people ask to attend. A personal link belongs to one guest and shows their own card, tickets and QR code.

**Does sending invitations from my own WhatsApp cost anything?**
No. The green WhatsApp icon opens your own WhatsApp and uses no credits. Only automatic WhatsApp sending uses credits.

**Why didn't an auto-approved guest get a WhatsApp message?**
Most likely the event had no WhatsApp credits left, or the event is on the Freemium plan. They still got their link on screen. Buy credits, then send their invitation from the guest list.

**Can I charge different contribution amounts to different guests?**
Yes. Set a **Contribution override** on a guest, or create a **guest group** with its own amount.

**Can a couple share one invitation?**
Yes. Set **Slots / tickets** to 2. The card then admits two people at the door.

**Can I edit my event the day before?**
Editing locks 48 hours before the event, so make final changes before then.

**Can I keep my event private?**
Yes. Set **Visibility** to **Private**. Only you can open the event page, and the personal links you send to guests still work.

**Can I change my event from a wedding to a traditional ceremony (or party)?**
Yes, but the invitation switches to that type's default template and your customised texts, colours and options are reset. Kezivents warns you first. Links you already shared keep working.

**Can I pay for my plan in instalments?**
Yes, by arrangement with the Kezivents team. You get a receipt for each payment showing your balance, and a friendly reminder when the balance is due (see [Plans](#2-plans-and-what-they-include)).

**A guest says they lost their invitation. What do I do?**
Share your event's **Guest code** with them (see [section 18](#18-guests-who-lost-their-invitation-link)), or copy their link from the guest list and send it again.

## 22. Glossary

- **Event code:** the short unique code for your event, for example BO-2026.
- **Event page:** the public version of your invitation, with the RSVP button when RSVP is on.
- **Personal invitation link:** one guest's own invitation and card.
- **Slots / tickets:** how many people a guest's card admits.
- **Ticket number:** a guest's 4-digit number (for example No. 1229), typed at the door when their QR code can't be scanned.
- **Hero section:** the top of the invitation, with your photos and names; the first thing guests see.
- **RSVP:** a request from a guest to attend, made through the form on your event page.
- **Auto-approval:** RSVPs are approved instantly without you reviewing them.
- **WhatsApp credit:** one automatic WhatsApp invitation. Credits belong to an event.
- **Manual WhatsApp:** sending an invitation from your own WhatsApp, for free.
- **Guest group:** a set of guests sharing the same tickets, contribution and card colour.
- **Guest invitation code:** a code you share so guests who lost their link can retrieve it.
- **Contribution:** the gift or contribution amount shown on a guest's card.
